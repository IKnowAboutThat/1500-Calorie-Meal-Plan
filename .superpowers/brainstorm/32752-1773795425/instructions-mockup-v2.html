Lemon Herb Grilled Chicken

Mediterranean Chicken Dinner Standard

A light, protein-packed grilled chicken with fresh herbs, bright lemon, and simple roasted vegetables. Ready in under 30 minutes.

  1. In a bowl, combine olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, and mixed herbs. Add chicken breast and marinate for at least 15 minutes.
  2. Preheat grill or grill pan to medium-high heat.
  3. Grill chicken for 6-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C). Let rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
  4. Meanwhile, toss asparagus with a drizzle of olive oil and grill for 3-4 minutes, turning occasionally.
  5. Halve the cherry tomatoes and arrange alongside the chicken and asparagus.
  6. Squeeze fresh lemon over everything and serve.
